# Frontend Dashboard V1.33

**Tanggal:** 15 Agustus 2026  
**Status:** Selesai  
**Fokus:** Motor & Drive Equipment Analysis Kalender

## Ditambahkan

- Klik motor/drive pada detail Kalender untuk membuka analisa equipment.
- Live cards: RMS Current, Voltage, Active Power, dan Drive Frequency.
- Historical cards: Runtime, Max RMS Amp, Energy, dan Unplanned Stop.
- Time range 1H, 8H, 24H, 7D; pilihan metric trend RMS Amp, Voltage, kW.
- Dragable historical trend dan navigator.
- Maintenance Target Plan serta recommendation berbasis kondisi.

## Batasan

- Data drive dan maintenance masih simulated dan read-only.
